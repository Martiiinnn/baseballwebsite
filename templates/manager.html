How this is calculated

Both pitchers are measured in expected wOBA (xwOBA). The starter's number is how hitters did the third time through in earlier regular-season starts that meet the filters above. The reliever's is projected from his own games, with each part pulled toward the league average. The difference, times the batters affected, divided by 1.2 gives runs; runs times leverage divided by 10 gives wins. A reliever must face at least three batters, while the starter could face one more and be reassessed: that option isn't modeled yet.

How this is calculated

The starter's number is how hitters did the third time through in 2018–2020 regular-season starts that met all three filters above. The reliever's numbers use only games before the date you pick. The difference in expected wOBA, times the batters affected, divided by 1.2 gives runs; runs times leverage divided by 10 gives wins. Handedness matchups aren't included here.

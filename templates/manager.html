How this is calculated

The rest of the half inning is solved exactly: every plate appearance, every base-out state, weighted by its chance. Each pitcher-hitter matchup combines the pitcher's and the hitter's rates for seven results (odds-ratio method, with the league's platoon effect). What each result does to outs, runners and runs, and the chance to win once the half inning ends, come from 2018–2020 games. Checked two ways: with league-average players it reproduces real run expectancy within 0.02 runs per inning and Statcast's win probability at the Game 6 moment.

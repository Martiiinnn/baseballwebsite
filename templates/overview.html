How the numbers are built
Data
Every pitch from the 2018–2020 regular seasons and the 2020 postseason (Statcast via pybaseball), loaded into SQLite and checked 10 ways before use, including matching Snell's official Game 6 box score.
Starter
Earlier regular-season starts about as dominant through two times, and the expected wOBA (xwOBA) hitters posted the third time through.
Reliever
Strikeout rate, walk rate and contact quality, each pulled toward the league average by an amount estimated from the data (60, 130 and 600 events). "Unchanged" uses all his games; "changed" uses only the postseason. The chance he has changed comes from how far his postseason strikeouts fell below what his history predicted against those hitters, starting from a 10% prior chosen by a backtest.
Win probability
xwOBA difference over 3 batters ÷ 1.2 = runs; runs × leverage ÷ 10 = wins. A game-state simulation is the planned replacement.

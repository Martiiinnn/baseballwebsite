How the numbers are built
Data
Every pitch from the 2018–2020 regular seasons and the 2020 postseason (Statcast via pybaseball), loaded into SQLite and checked 10 ways before use, including matching Snell's official Game 6 box score.
Starter
Regular-season starts where the pitcher was at least this dominant through two times, and how hitters did the third time through.
Reliever
Three versions of Anderson: full strength (2019–20 regular seasons), recent form (this postseason blended with full strength) and recent form taken at face value. Small samples are blended with 300 plate appearances of the baseline.
Win probability
Expected wOBA difference over 3 batters ÷ 1.2 = runs; runs × leverage ÷ 10 = wins. Accounting for each hitter's handedness gives nearly the same answer (+1.7 / +0.7 / −4.7).
